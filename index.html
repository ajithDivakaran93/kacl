KACL Happiness Index™️

More Rewards. More Recognition. More Reasons to Smile.

The KACL Happiness Index™️ is our commitment to making KACL Grand Season 4 more rewarding for both players and franchise owners.

Rather than measuring success only through match results and trophies, we create additional opportunities for participants to earn rewards, receive recognition, and enjoy memorable experiences throughout the tournament.

How Does It Work?

01

Every Performance Counts

Players have opportunities to earn rewards for individual achievements, including catches, Man of the Match awards, and outstanding performances with the bat, ball, or behind the stumps.

02

Every Milestone Matters

From league victories to knockout-stage achievements, the tournament recognizes important moments with meaningful rewards for players and franchise owners.

03

Every Participant Matters

The Happiness Index extends beyond the winning team. It is designed to recognize individual excellence, reward team achievements, and make participation more valuable for everyone involved.

04

Partners Make More Possible

Through brand partnerships, the Happiness Index creates opportunities for additional rewards, products, experiences, and special recognitions—connecting sponsors with players and owners in meaningful ways.

Our Philosophy

The Happiness Index is not simply about how much prize money is distributed. It is about how many opportunities we create to celebrate achievement, reward effort, and make the tournament memorable.

At KACL, the trophy celebrates the winner. The Happiness Index celebrates the people who make the game special.

Measuring success by the smiles we create.
