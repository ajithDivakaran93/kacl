Before you register

GOOD TO KNOW.

Who can register as a player?

Eligibility is defined by KACL's Player Eligibility Policy, including past division, district/state, and professional tournament participation criteria. Final eligibility is subject to verification and approval by the KACL Organizing Committee. Please review the official registration rules before paying.

What is the player registration fee?

₹5,999 including GST, according to the supplied Season 4 player invitation.

Is player insurance included?

The brochure describes proposed player welfare arrangements, including group personal accident insurance (preferably ₹5 lakh per player), medical coverage during official matches if available, and public liability insurance. These are proposals, not a confirmation of issued coverage.

How does the registration button work?

Registration is completed on the external KACL website. This landing page does not collect personal details or payments.
